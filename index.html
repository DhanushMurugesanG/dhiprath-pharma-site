About us

A new pharmaceutical company built on old-fashioned discipline.

Dhiprath Pharma Private Limited is a recently established pharmaceutical company based in Bengaluru, Karnataka. The company was incorporated on 16 May 2026 and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Bangalore.

As a new-age pharmaceutical organisation, we are committed to delivering quality healthcare products and solutions through strong partnerships, ethical practices and customer satisfaction.

Our focus is on building robust systems, regulatory compliance and operational excellence — to enable sustainable growth and create value for our stakeholders.

We do not ask hospitals to take our word for it. Every product we carry comes from a named, authorised principal, and every consignment is traceable to a licensed supply chain.

  • Quality & Compliance

    Committed to quality products and full regulatory compliance under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act.

  • Strong Partnerships

    Collaborating with trusted and reputed pharmaceutical companies as an authorised partner.

  • Customer Focus

    Delivering reliable healthcare solutions with integrity — and answering the phone when it rings.
